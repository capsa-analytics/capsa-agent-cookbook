# Visit rescheduled

**What good looks like.** The customer hears about a move before they notice it.
A move of three or more days, or any move on a property tagged call-ahead,
deserves a message the same day. Mention the new date, why it moved when we
know (weather, crew, materials), and what, if anything, they need to do.

**When nothing is needed.** The whole route moved the same day (many properties
show the same shift): record **Not actionable – Part of a bulk schedule change**
unless the property is call-ahead or the customer asked for the original date.
A move of one or two days on a routine service needs no message.

**When to escalate.** The same visit has moved twice or more in two weeks: tell
the ops manager and assign work so someone owns the new date.

**Who to contact.** The property's primary contact. For HOA properties, the
board or management contact on file, not residents.
