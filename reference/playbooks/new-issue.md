# New issue

**What good looks like.** The customer knows we saw it within one business day.
If the issue came from the customer, acknowledge it and say who owns it and
when they will hear back. If a crew logged it, decide whether the customer needs
to know before work starts (damage, access, safety: yes; internal quality note:
usually no).

**When nothing is needed.** The issue is internal and already assigned with a
due date: **Not actionable – Crew already handling it**.

**When to escalate.** Any complaint on a property that is already At risk, or a
second complaint in thirty days: assign work to the account owner for a call,
due in one business day.

**Who to contact.** Whoever raised it; for crew-logged issues, the primary
contact.
