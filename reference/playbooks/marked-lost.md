# Marked lost

**What good looks like.** We learn why within two business days, from the
customer, not from guessing. Call the primary contact; ask what drove the
decision (price, service, scope, a competitor), and whether a revised scope or
timing would have changed it. Record what you heard as a note, even when the
answer is "no reason given".

**When nothing is needed.** The contract was replaced by a new one we won
(renewal restructured) or the property was sold: **Not actionable – Already
resolved in Aspire** with a line saying which.

**When to escalate.** Annual value above the branch threshold, or a lost
contract at a property with other active work: assign work to the branch
manager for a save attempt, due in three business days.

**Who to contact.** The decision maker on the account, not the site contact.
