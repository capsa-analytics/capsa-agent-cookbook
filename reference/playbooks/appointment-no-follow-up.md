# Appointment with no follow-up recorded

**What good looks like.** Within a day of a customer meeting or site walk, there
is a note on the property saying what was discussed and agreed, and a task or
issue for each commitment we made. If the meeting produced a quote request,
the proposal is started. Record **Noted** with the summary; assign work for the
commitments from the row.

**When nothing is needed.** The meeting did not happen, was internal, or was a
calendar hold: **Not actionable** with the reason (Duplicate, Not
customer-facing, or your own words). A meeting that produced nothing to do is
still worth one line: **Noted – "Quarterly check-in, no changes requested."**

**When to escalate.** A site walk with the decision maker on a property that is
At risk: tell the branch manager the same day.

**Who to contact.** Nobody by default. This update is about our own record, not
a customer message.
