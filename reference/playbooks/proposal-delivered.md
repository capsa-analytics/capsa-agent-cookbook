# Proposal delivered

**What good looks like.** Every delivered proposal gets a human follow-up within
five business days: confirm they received it, ask whether anything is unclear,
offer a walk-through. Record the contact with the channel and day. If the
customer answered already, record **Noted** with what they said.

**When nothing is needed.** The customer asked us not to follow up before a
given date: **Noted** with that date, and let the proposal follow-up schedule
carry the next touch.

**When to escalate.** No answer after two touches: assign work to the sales rep
to call, due in two business days.

**Who to contact.** The person the proposal was addressed to.
